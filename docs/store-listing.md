## Short description

Your Elite Dangerous companion: live mining, combat, trade and engineering statistics, read from the game's journal, on your computer, with nothing sent anywhere.

## Description

EDRockMaster Companion follows your Elite Dangerous sessions as you play. It reads the journal the game writes on your computer and shows, live, what you earn and how fast, whether you mine, fight, trade or engineer your ship.

Mining: prospector alerts for the commodities and contents you choose, cores and motherlodes, tons refined, limpets used, cargo and sales, credits per hour.

Combat: kills, bounties and combat bonds by site (conflict zones in space and on the ground, resource extraction sites, navigation beacons), kills and credits per hour by site type, unredeemed vouchers, fines, community goals.

Trade: profit and profit per hour of flight, every route with its commodity and markets, cargo bought, losses, transfers with fleet carriers (a deposit the game did not take shows at once).

Engineering: your materials by category and grade with their caps, the engineers you have met, and your blueprint goals: what each one still misses, which unlocked engineer offers it, and the shopping list of everything you need. Rolls made at an engineer count your goals down.

Your situation at a glance: commander, ship, system and station, game mode and private group, wing.

Everything is computed on your computer. No account, no advertising, no tracking: the application sends nothing. It does not change the game, and plays nothing for you: it only reads the journal the game writes for tools like it.

Works alongside EDMarketConnector, or without it. In English and French.
